    Ziegler–Nichols tuning

    The plant and sensor stay fixed. In the P-only test, increase Kp until the peaks stay roughly the same height. Compare 2.5, 3 and 3.5.

    For this loop the characteristic polynomial is s³ + 2s² + 2s + (1 + Kp). At Ku = 3 it factors as (s + 2)(s² + 2). Compare the measured period with 2π/√2 ≈ 4.44 s.
